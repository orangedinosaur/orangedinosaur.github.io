Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 11, 2026  |  Last Updated: October 11, 2026

Neiroha Labs LLC ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Mathlet mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our App.

Information We Collect

Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Mathlet stores your profiles and practice history locally on your device. This includes:

  • Profiles: The name and avatar you choose for each profile
  • Practice History: The date and time of each practice, the questions asked, the answers given, whether each answer was correct, and how long each answer took
  • Progress: Facts learned, speed, and practice streaks, worked out from the practice history
  • Practice Settings: Session length, time limit, answer method (keypad or voice), chosen times tables, number ranges, and speed goals
  • App Preferences: Settings such as appearance, answer sounds, undo, and missed-facts focus

This data is stored in a local database on your device. It is not transmitted to our servers and is not synced to iCloud.

Voice Answers

If you turn on voice answers, Mathlet listens through the microphone so you can say answers out loud. Speech is turned into numbers entirely on your device using Apple's on-device speech recognition. Your voice is never recorded, stored, or sent anywhere, including to us.

Information Collected Automatically

When you use the App, we automatically collect anonymous usage information to help us understand how Mathlet is used and to improve it:

Analytics Data (via Mixpanel)

  • An anonymous Installation ID (a randomly generated identifier unique to your app installation)
  • A randomly generated identifier for the active profile, and the number of profiles on the device
  • App usage events, such as starting, completing, or leaving a practice, viewing progress, and changing a setting
  • Practice summaries, such as the operation, session length, time limit, and counts of correct and incorrect answers
  • Subscription events, such as viewing the upgrade screen, the plan chosen, and whether a purchase succeeded
  • Whether voice permission was granted, and the general reason when voice answers are unavailable
  • General device information, such as device type (iPhone or iPad), device model, operating system version, and app version

What we never collect: profile names, avatars, the specific questions or answers, voice audio, your name, email address, contacts, or precise location. We have turned off Mixpanel's IP-based location lookup, and we do not link analytics data to your identity.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide App Functionality: Store practice history and show progress on your device
  • Improve the App: Analyze anonymous usage patterns to understand which features are most valuable
  • Fix Issues: Spot problems such as data that failed to save or voice answers that could not start

We do not show ads, we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we do not sell your information.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

Mixpanel (Analytics)

We use Mixpanel to collect the anonymous usage analytics described above. Mixpanel receives your anonymous Installation ID and app usage events. For more information, see Mixpanel's Privacy Policy.

Apple (Purchases and Speech Recognition)

Mathlet PRO subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, Apple Account, or payment details; the App only checks with Apple whether PRO is active on your device. Voice answers use Apple's on-device speech recognition, which runs on your device. Apple's handling of your data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Data Storage and Security

  • Local Storage: Profiles, practice history, progress, and settings are stored locally on your device
  • No Upload to Our Servers: We do not upload your profiles or practice history to our servers
  • No Cloud Sync: Mathlet does not sync data to iCloud or any other cloud service
  • Data Deletion: You can delete a practice session or a profile within the App. All locally stored data is deleted when you uninstall the App
  • Anonymous Analytics: Analytics data sent to Mixpanel uses only randomly generated identifiers

Device Permissions

The App may request the following permissions, only if you choose to use voice answers:

  • Microphone: To listen for spoken answers during practice
  • Speech Recognition: To turn spoken answers into numbers on your device

Mathlet works fully with the on-screen keypad if you do not grant these permissions. You can manage them at any time through your device's Settings.

Contacting Us by Email

If you send feedback from the App, your email app opens a message to us that includes the App version, iOS version, and device model, to help us look into your message. We use your email address and message only to reply to you.

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Profile names, avatars, and practice history stay on the device, voice answers are processed on the device and never recorded, and the analytics we collect are anonymous.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the email address below, and we will take steps to delete such information.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access Your Data: All profiles and practice history are stored locally on your device and accessible within the App
  • Delete Your Data: You can delete individual practice sessions or profiles within the App, or delete all data by uninstalling the App
  • Ask Questions: You can contact us at any time with questions or requests about your data

Data Retention

  • Local Data: Retained on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App
  • Analytics Data: Retained by Mixpanel according to their data retention policies

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within the App or on our website. Changes are effective when they are posted.
